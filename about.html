Our Story

A haven born from a belief in guidance, comfort, and the power of sharing your story.

About BurkeHaven Counseling

BurkeHaven Counseling exists to provide mental health and behavioral services for families in the metro area. Our goal is to help families and individuals find a haven of peace and safety in their lives.

Our Philosophy

There are times in all our lives when we need to reach out to someone. When we cry out inside for help, to find a helper, someone to walk beside us, offer guidance and comfort on our journey. As individuals, partners, or parents.

Everyone has a story. I invite you to come, to share your story. Reach out for that helping, guiding hand. I would love to be a part of your story.

About Sandra Thomas-Tyler, LPC

Education & Credentials

Early Childhood Education Studies, BS in Family Studies, MA in Counseling Psychology. Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor through the State of Oklahoma. Trained for children aged 0–5.

Specialized Training

TF-CBT, Trust Based Relational Intervention (TBRI), Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT), Circle of Security, and specialized training in Children of Divorce, Deportation, Deployment, and Death, plus childhood and adult grief.

Passion & Experience

Passionate about being with parents in their pain, promise, and potential. Experience mentoring young mothers, assisting courts with single parents, foster, and adoptive families. Dedicated to helping adults and children succeed professionally, academically, socially, and emotionally.
