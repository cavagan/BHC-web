Services

Thoughtful therapeutic counseling for individuals, families, and parents.

Therapeutic Counseling

In-person and virtual sessions. Most major insurances accepted, cash payment options available, and a free initial consultation.

Individual

One-on-one support for anxiety, depression, life transitions, self-esteem, and personal growth.

Family & Couples

Guidance to strengthen communication, navigate conflict, and restore connection within relationships and the family system.

Children & Youth

Specialized care for children ages 0–6 and youth 7–18, including infant mental health services.

Parent Education

Practical tools and courses to support parents in building stronger, more peaceful relationships with their children.

Specialty Services

P.C.I.T.

Parent-Child Interaction Therapy — a specialized course for parents of strong-willed children to strengthen their relationship and encourage peace in the home.

Circle of Security

A state-approved parenting course that teaches basic parenting skills for families at all stages.

TF-CBT

Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. Trauma-informed care for individuals who have experienced a traumatic event.

Court Ordered Services

Reunification therapy, supervised visitation, parenting classes, and support for crime victims.

LPC Candidate Supervision

Professional supervision for LPC candidates seeking licensure support.
