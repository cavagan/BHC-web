BurkeHaven Counseling LLC

Brand Essence

BurkeHaven Counseling is a peaceful haven for families and individuals seeking guidance, healing, and strength. The brand centers on the idea of a safe, welcoming cabin — a place of warmth, shelter, and restoration. Messaging is warm, personal, professional, and family-centered.

Tagline / Positioning: “A haven of peace and safety.” Helping families and individuals share their story and find a guiding hand.

Logo

The primary mark is the hand-drawn cabin with tree and chimney smoke, paired with “BurkeHaven” in elegant script and “Counseling” (or “Counseling LLC”) beneath. Use the transparent PNG version on light backgrounds and the solid versions where contrast is needed.

  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the cabin around the logo.
  • Do not stretch, recolor, or add effects.
  • Minimum size: 180px wide for digital.

Navy is the dominant brand color. Use cream and white for backgrounds. Warm gold accent sparingly for lines or highlights.

Typography

  • Headlines & Logo Text: Great Vibes (script) — elegant, personal, matches the hand-drawn logo.
  • Subheads: Playfair Display — refined serif for structure.
  • Body: Inter — clean, highly readable sans-serif.

Voice & Tone

Warm, inviting, professional, and compassionate. Speak in first person when sharing Sandra’s story. Emphasize safety, guidance, family, and the invitation to “share your story.” Avoid clinical jargon unless explaining a specific modality.

Key Messaging

  • Everyone has a story. We invite you to share yours.
  • A haven of peace and safety for families and individuals.
  • In-person and virtual sessions available.
  • Most major insurances accepted • Cash options • Free initial consultation.

Photography & Illustration

Prefer the official cabin line-art logo and authentic office/family imagery. When generating new visuals, stay within the navy + cream palette and warm, welcoming, family-oriented mood. Avoid overly abstract or mismatched nature aesthetics.
