Welcome to BurkeHaven

BurkeHaven Counseling exists to provide mental health and behavioral services for families in the metro area. Our goal is to help families and individuals find a haven of peace and safety in their lives.

Individual & Family Care

Support for individuals, couples, parents, children (0–6), and youth (7–18) in a safe, welcoming environment.

Specialized Therapies

PCIT, Circle of Security, TF-CBT, court-ordered services, and more — tailored to strengthen relationships and restore peace.

Flexible & Accessible

In-person and virtual sessions. Most major insurances accepted, cash options available, and a free initial consultation.

Owned & Operated with Care

BurkeHaven Counseling LLC is independently owned and operated by Sandra Thomas-Tyler, LPC. We are here to walk beside you, offer guidance, and help you write the next chapter of your story.
