Stories of Healing

Anonymized reflections from families and individuals who have found a haven at BurkeHaven.

Case Studies

These composite stories illustrate common journeys. Individual results vary; every path is unique. Names and details have been changed to protect privacy.

Finding Ground After Burnout

Client: Adult professional, mid-30s

“I arrived exhausted and disconnected from myself. The sessions felt like stepping into a quiet, safe space — enough to rest and then slowly rebuild.”

Through individual counseling focused on mindfulness and boundary-setting, the client reported reduced anxiety, improved sleep, and a renewed sense of agency within three months.

Reconnecting as Partners

Clients: Couple, together 8 years

“We learned to listen without defending. The space felt non-judgmental, which made hard conversations possible.”

Couples sessions helped identify patterns of withdrawal and criticism. Using structured communication tools, they described feeling more emotionally available and hopeful about their future together.

Strengthening the Parent-Child Bond

Clients: Parent and strong-willed child

“PCIT gave us practical tools and a way to connect that actually worked at home. The peace we found was real.”

Through Parent-Child Interaction Therapy, the family learned skills to strengthen their relationship, reduce conflict, and encourage lasting peace in the home.
